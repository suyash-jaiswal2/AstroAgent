# House 8: Transformation, Secrets, and Inheritance

The 8th house governs death, transformation, hidden knowledge, inheritance, occult sciences, and the mysteries of life. It is the most intense and karmic house — associated with profound change and the dissolution of the old.

Natural ruler: Mars/Pluto (Scorpio). Body: Reproductive organs, genitals, excretory system. Vedic name: Ayu Bhava (house of longevity).

Themes: death and rebirth, longevity, inheritance, taxes, insurance, occult knowledge, hidden assets, transformative crises, and sexual energy. The 8th house also governs research, investigation, and knowledge of hidden things.

A strong 8th house lord can indicate longevity, inheritance wealth, and interest in the occult. Benefics in the 8th (especially Jupiter) often protect longevity. Malefics here can shorten life or bring sudden reversals but also indicate deep spiritual power.

Saturn in the 8th house is considered a classic indicator of long life. Rahu in the 8th intensifies all 8th house themes and often indicates a fascination with occult matters or sudden life upheavals.

Key phrases: death, transformation, inheritance, longevity, occult, secrets, Ayu Bhava, Scorpio, sudden change, 8th lord.
