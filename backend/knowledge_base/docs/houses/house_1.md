# House 1: The Ascendant — Self, Body, and First Impressions

The 1st house (Lagna or Ascendant) is the most important house in the chart. It represents the self, physical body, personality, and the lens through which we engage with the world. The rising sign determines the chart ruler, which becomes the most important planet in the Vedic chart.

Natural ruler: Mars (Aries). Body: Head, brain, complexion. Vedic name: Tanu Bhava (house of the body).

Themes: physical appearance, personality, temperament, vitality, early childhood, overall life direction, and how others perceive us at first meeting.

Planets in the 1st house strongly color the entire personality. Jupiter here bestows wisdom and optimism. Saturn brings seriousness and discipline. Mars creates assertiveness and drive. Sun gives confidence. Moon makes the person emotionally sensitive and changeable.

The Ascendant degree and sign set the tone for the entire chart. Sagittarius rising produces the philosophical adventurer; Scorpio rising, the intense investigator; Libra rising, the social diplomat.

Key phrases: self, body, personality, Ascendant, Lagna, chart ruler, first impressions, physical vitality.
