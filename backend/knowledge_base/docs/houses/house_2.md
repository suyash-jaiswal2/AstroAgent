# House 2: Wealth, Speech, and Family

The 2nd house governs accumulated wealth, speech, family of origin, food, and the face. It represents what we possess and how we communicate our values. In Vedic astrology, the 2nd house lord's placement is crucial for financial analysis.

Natural ruler: Venus (Taurus). Body: Face, mouth, throat, right eye. Vedic name: Dhana Bhava (house of wealth).

Themes: money, savings, accumulated resources, family traditions, food preferences, speech quality, values, and self-worth. The 2nd house is part of the Dhana (wealth) axis along with the 11th house.

Jupiter in the 2nd house is one of the strongest indicators of wealth and eloquence. Venus here beautifies speech and brings financial ease. Saturn in the 2nd delays financial accumulation but often produces lasting wealth through sustained effort.

Speech is a 2nd house matter — the quality, truthfulness, and impact of one's words are shown here. Afflictions to the 2nd house can indicate financial instability, dishonesty in speech, or difficult family dynamics.

Key phrases: wealth, speech, family, food, savings, values, 2nd lord, Dhana Bhava, throat.
