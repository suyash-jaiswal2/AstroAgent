# House 4: Home, Mother, and Inner Happiness

The 4th house is the foundation of the chart — it represents home, mother, emotional security, property, education, and the inner state of peace and contentment. It is the most private and deeply personal house.

Natural ruler: Moon (Cancer). Body: Chest, heart, lungs, stomach. Vedic name: Sukha Bhava (house of happiness).

Themes: mother, home and property, vehicles, education (especially foundational), emotional peace, the subconscious, and one's homeland. The IC (Imum Coeli) in Western astrology corresponds to the 4th house cusp.

Jupiter in the 4th house is one of the finest placements for inner happiness, a blessed home environment, and a nurturing mother. Moon in the 4th (its natural house) creates a strong connection to home and emotional depth. Saturn in the 4th can delay home ownership but produces lasting stability.

The 4th house and its lord significantly influence the experience of one's homeland, whether one lives near or far from birthplace, and the emotional legacy passed down through family.

Key phrases: home, mother, property, happiness, emotional security, education, vehicles, IC, Sukha Bhava.
