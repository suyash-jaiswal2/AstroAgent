# House 3: Siblings, Communication, and Courage

The 3rd house governs siblings, communication, short journeys, courage, and self-expression. It represents the power of action and the immediate environment — the neighborhood, local community, and daily communication.

Natural ruler: Mercury (Gemini). Body: Arms, shoulders, hands, ears, lungs. Vedic name: Sahaja Bhava (house of siblings and effort).

Themes: younger siblings, neighbors, short-distance travel, writing, media, courage (parakrama), hobbies, skills, and the ability to take initiative and exert effort.

A strong 3rd house with benefic influence indicates excellent communication skills, supportive siblings, writing ability, and personal courage. Mars in the 3rd house is particularly powerful for courage and physical effort. Mercury here enhances writing, speaking, and analytical skills.

The 3rd house is an upachaya (growth) house — planets here generally improve over time with effort. Malefic planets like Mars in the 3rd can actually be beneficial, granting courage and competitive drive.

Key phrases: siblings, communication, courage, short travel, writing, media, hands, Sahaja Bhava, upachaya.
