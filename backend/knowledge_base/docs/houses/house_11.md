# House 11: Gains, Friends, and Aspirations

The 11th house is the house of gains (Labha) — it governs income, fulfillment of desires, friends and networks, elder siblings, and the realization of long-cherished goals. It is the most powerful upachaya house.

Natural ruler: Saturn (Aquarius). Body: Calves, ankles, left ear. Vedic name: Labha Bhava (house of gains).

Themes: income, financial gains, friends and social networks, elder siblings, hopes and dreams, humanitarian causes, large organizations, and the completion of desires. The 11th house is also associated with the marketplace and trade.

Saturn in the 11th house is considered highly auspicious for material gains — slow but consistent accumulation. Jupiter here brings wealth through wisdom and generous networks. Rahu in the 11th is one of the best Rahu placements for material success and influential connections.

The 11th lord's strength is key to understanding one's financial flow and social circle. The connection between the 2nd house (accumulated wealth), 10th house (career), and 11th house (income flow) reveals the complete financial picture.

Key phrases: gains, income, friends, aspirations, Labha Bhava, elder sibling, desires fulfilled, upachaya, networks.
