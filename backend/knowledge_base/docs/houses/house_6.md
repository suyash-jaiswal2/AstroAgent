# House 6: Health, Service, and Enemies

The 6th house governs health, daily work, service, enemies, obstacles, and the capacity to overcome difficulties. It is a Dusthana (difficult) house but also an upachaya — it improves with time and is powerful for competitive success.

Natural ruler: Mercury (Virgo). Body: Intestines, lower abdomen, digestive system. Vedic name: Ari Bhava (house of enemies).

Themes: health and illness, daily work routines, service and duty, employees and subordinates, debts, legal disputes, enemies, and pets. The 6th house also governs the ability to compete, fight back, and overcome adversity.

Mars in the 6th house is a powerful placement — it fights off enemies, overcomes obstacles, and creates competitive success. Saturn here produces a disciplined worker with the ability to endure. Jupiter in the 6th can sometimes indicate overindulgence that leads to health issues.

Planets in the 6th house, while difficult in personal life matters, can be extremely powerful for professionals in healing, law, and service fields.

Key phrases: health, service, enemies, debts, daily work, obstacles, digestion, Ari Bhava, upachaya, competition.
