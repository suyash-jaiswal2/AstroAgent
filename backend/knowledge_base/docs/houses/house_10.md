# House 10: Career, Status, and Public Life

The 10th house is the house of karma — action, career, public reputation, social status, and the role one plays in the world. It is one of the four angular (kendra) houses and among the most powerful positions in the chart.

Natural ruler: Saturn (Capricorn). Body: Knees, bones of the back. Vedic name: Karma Bhava (house of action).

Themes: career and profession, public life and status, authority figures, government, fame, achievement, and the midheaven (MC). The 10th house and its lord describe how one is seen in the world and the nature of one's life work.

Sun in the 10th house is one of the most powerful career placements — it creates natural authority and visibility. Saturn in its own house (Capricorn/Aquarius 10th) often produces exceptional career longevity and respect. Jupiter in the 10th brings career through wisdom, teaching, or counseling.

The 10th from the Moon (Chandra Lagna) and from the Sun are also analyzed. Planets aspecting the 10th house are as important as planets within it.

Key phrases: career, status, Midheaven MC, Karma Bhava, public life, authority, achievement, Saturn, 10th lord.
