# House 7: Partnership, Marriage, and Business

The 7th house governs all one-to-one relationships — marriage, business partnerships, open enemies, and significant others. It is directly opposite the Ascendant, representing the other in our life.

Natural ruler: Venus (Libra). Body: Lower abdomen, reproductive organs, kidneys. Vedic name: Kalatra Bhava (house of spouse).

Themes: marriage, spouse qualities, business partners, open enemies, diplomatic negotiations, sexual relationships, and contracts. The 7th house lord and planets within it describe the nature of key partnerships.

Jupiter in the 7th brings a wise, generous spouse and harmonious partnerships. Venus here (in its natural house) is powerful for marriage and relationships. Saturn in the 7th often delays marriage but can indicate a lasting, mature partnership. Mars in the 7th (Mangal Dosha position) requires careful matching.

In Vedic astrology, the 7th house from the Moon (Chandra Lagna) and Venus placement are equally important for marriage analysis. Upapada Lagna (derived from the 12th lord) gives additional spouse information.

Key phrases: marriage, spouse, business partner, 7th lord, Kalatra Bhava, Venus, contracts, diplomacy.
