# Hamsa Yoga — Panchavargiya Mahapurusha Yoga

Hamsa Yoga is one of the five Mahapurusha Yogas (Great Person Yogas) — it forms when Jupiter is placed in a kendra house (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th) in its own sign (Sagittarius or Pisces) or exaltation (Cancer).

Hamsa (the divine swan) represents spiritual discernment, wisdom, and purity. The native with Hamsa Yoga possesses extraordinary wisdom, moral rectitude, and spiritual authority. They are often teachers, spiritual leaders, counselors, or philosophers.

Physical characteristics traditionally associated: fair complexion, good proportions, a melodious voice. Character: virtuous, dharmic, compassionate.

This yoga is fully activated during Jupiter's Mahadasha or Antardasha, and is particularly powerful when Jupiter is unafflicted and strongly placed.

Key phrases: Hamsa Yoga, Jupiter Mahapurusha, kendra, own sign, exaltation, wisdom, spirituality, dharma.
