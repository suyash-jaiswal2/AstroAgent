# Malavya Yoga — Venus Mahapurusha

Malavya Yoga forms when Venus is in a kendra house (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th) in its own sign (Taurus or Libra) or exaltation (Pisces at 27°).

This is one of the five Mahapurusha Yogas and produces exceptional grace, beauty, artistic talent, and material luxury. The native is magnetic, elegant, charming, and often achieves success through artistic or diplomatic channels.

Malavya Yoga natives are known for their refined aesthetic sensibility, pleasure in luxury, harmonious relationships, and natural attractiveness. The yoga is most powerful when Venus is unafflicted and the Ascendant lord is also well-placed.

Activation: During Venus Mahadasha (20 years) or Venus Antardasha, the yoga's gifts fully manifest.

Key phrases: Malavya Yoga, Venus Mahapurusha, kendra, Taurus, Libra, Pisces, beauty, grace, luxury, art.
