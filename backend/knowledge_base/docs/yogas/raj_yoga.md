# Raj Yoga — Royal Combination for Power and Success

Raj Yoga is formed when a kendra lord (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house lord) and a trikona lord (1st, 5th, or 9th house lord) are in mutual conjunction, aspect, or exchange (Parivartana).

The 1st house is both a kendra and trikona, making its lord the most powerful for Raj Yoga formation. A person with a strong Raj Yoga achieves positions of authority, social prominence, and material success proportional to the yoga's strength.

Strength of Raj Yoga depends on: the strength of the planets involved, their house placements, freedom from affliction (no conjunction with Saturn/Rahu/Ketu without benefic influence), and activation during the relevant Dasha.

Key phrases: Raj Yoga, kendra lord, trikona lord, power, success, authority, Dasha activation.
