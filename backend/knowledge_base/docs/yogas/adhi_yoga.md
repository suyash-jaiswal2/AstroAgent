# Adhi Yoga — The Minister's Yoga

Adhi Yoga forms when Jupiter, Mercury, and Venus (individually or collectively) are placed in the 6th, 7th, and 8th houses from the Moon. This combination in the sensitive houses creates an advisorial, strategic, and influential personality.

The native with Adhi Yoga tends toward advisory, diplomatic, or ministerial roles. They possess the analytical power of Mercury, the wisdom of Jupiter, and the grace of Venus — making them excellent counselors, diplomats, or behind-the-scenes influencers.

The yoga is strongest when all three planets are present in these houses from the Moon, and when they are in their own signs, exaltation, or friendly signs. Even one planet in the right position creates a partial Adhi Yoga.

Key phrases: Adhi Yoga, Jupiter Mercury Venus, 6th 7th 8th from Moon, advisor, minister, diplomat, counselor.
