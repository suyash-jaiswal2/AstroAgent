# Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga — Cancellation of Debilitation

When a planet is debilitated (in its sign of weakness) but the debilitation is cancelled by specific conditions, the result is Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga — often producing extraordinary success precisely from the area where weakness seemed certain.

Cancellation conditions: the lord of the debilitation sign is in a kendra from the Ascendant or Moon; the planet that exalts in the debilitation sign is in a kendra; the debilitated planet itself is in a kendra; or the planet exchanges signs with its debilitation sign lord.

For example: Saturn debilitated in Aries is cancelled if Mars (Aries ruler) is in a kendra, or if the Sun (exalted in Aries) is in a kendra. This cancelled debilitation often produces a compensating drive for mastery.

Key phrases: Neecha Bhanga, debilitation cancellation, Raja Yoga, compensation, mastery from weakness, kendra.
