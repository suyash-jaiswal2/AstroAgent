# Budhaditya Yoga — Sun and Mercury United

Budhaditya Yoga forms when Mercury (Budha) and the Sun (Aditya) are in the same house in the natal chart. This is one of the most common yogas and enhances intellectual brilliance, communication skills, and professional success through the power of the mind.

Mercury within approximately 15°–20° of the Sun (not combust, which is within 3–4°) creates the most effective form of this yoga. When Mercury is combust (within 3° of the Sun), the yoga's effects are reduced, as Mercury's energy merges too completely with solar heat.

The strength of Budhaditya Yoga depends on the sign and house it occupies. In Virgo (where Mercury is exalted), it becomes exceptionally powerful. In Libra (Sun's debilitation sign), the effects are more moderate.

Effects: sharp analytical mind, eloquent speech, professional success through intellect, writing ability, and often government or administrative success.

Key phrases: Budhaditya Yoga, Sun Mercury conjunction, intellect, communication, Virgo, professional success.
