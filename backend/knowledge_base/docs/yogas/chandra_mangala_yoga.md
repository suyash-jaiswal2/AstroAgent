# Chandra Mangala Yoga — Moon and Mars United

Chandra Mangala Yoga forms when the Moon and Mars are in conjunction, mutual aspect, or in the 1st/7th axis from each other. This combination of the mind (Moon) with drive and energy (Mars) creates an enterprising, emotionally motivated, and commercially savvy personality.

Effects: strong business instincts, emotional courage, earning through independent enterprise, and a tendency toward passionate pursuit of goals. The native often has an entrepreneurial spirit and the emotional resilience to sustain competitive effort.

When well-placed (in a kendra or trikona, in friendly signs), this yoga produces financial independence and business success. When afflicted, it can indicate emotional impulsiveness or confrontational behavior.

Key phrases: Chandra Mangala, Moon Mars conjunction, business, entrepreneurial, emotional courage, enterprise.
