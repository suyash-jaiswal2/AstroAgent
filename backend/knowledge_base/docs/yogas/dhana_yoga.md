# Dhana Yoga — Wealth Combinations

Dhana Yogas (wealth combinations) form when the lords of the wealth-giving houses (2nd, 5th, 9th, and 11th) are in conjunction, mutual aspect, or exchange with each other, or when they occupy these houses themselves.

The most powerful Dhana Yogas involve: 2nd and 11th lords connected (basic wealth), 5th and 9th lords connected (trikona-trikona — immense fortune through past karma), and any of these lords conjunct in a kendra with a Raj Yoga planet.

Jupiter and Venus naturally participate in Dhana Yogas by their benefic nature. Their placement in the 2nd or 11th house adds financial grace. The 9th lord in the 2nd or 11th is one of the most consistent indicators of significant wealth.

Key phrases: Dhana Yoga, 2nd lord, 5th lord, 9th lord, 11th lord, wealth, fortune, exchange, trikona.
