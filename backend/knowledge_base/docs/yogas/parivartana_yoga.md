# Parivartana Yoga — Planetary Exchange

Parivartana Yoga (exchange yoga) forms when two planets occupy each other's signs — for example, Jupiter in Scorpio (Mars's sign) and Mars in Sagittarius (Jupiter's sign). Each planet exchanges dignity, creating a powerful mutual support.

There are three types: Maha Yoga (between two kendra/trikona lords — most auspicious), Dainya Yoga (involving 6th, 8th, or 12th lords — creates challenges), and Kahala Yoga (between the 4th and 9th lords).

The planets involved in Parivartana effectively act as if they are in their own signs, gaining dignity and mutual strength. Raj Yoga Parivartana between trikona and kendra lords is among the most powerful positive yogas.

Key phrases: Parivartana, exchange, mutual sign, Maha Yoga, Dainya Yoga, Kahala Yoga, dignity, mutual support.
