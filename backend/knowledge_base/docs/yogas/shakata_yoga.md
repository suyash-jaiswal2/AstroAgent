# Shakata Yoga — The Wheel of Fortune

Shakata Yoga forms when the Moon is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house from Jupiter. It is traditionally associated with unstable fortune — the native's life rises and falls like a wheel, experiencing alternating periods of prosperity and reversal.

The yoga is cancelled if the Moon is in a kendra from the Ascendant. Cancellation significantly mitigates the wheel-like instability and adds resilience to fortune.

Shakata Yoga's primary lesson is non-attachment to outcomes and the development of equanimity through life's inevitable cycles. Spiritually, it can be a profound teacher.

Key phrases: Shakata Yoga, Moon from Jupiter, 6th, 8th, 12th, unstable fortune, wheel, cancellation.
