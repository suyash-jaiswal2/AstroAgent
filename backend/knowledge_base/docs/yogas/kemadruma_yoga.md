# Kemadruma Yoga — The Solitary Moon

Kemadruma Yoga forms when the Moon has no planets in the 2nd or 12th house from it (adjacent houses), and no planets conjunct it. This is traditionally considered a challenging yoga indicating struggle, poverty of support, and a sense of emotional isolation.

However, Kemadruma Yoga is cancelled (Bhangi) by: a planet in the 1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house from the Moon, a planet conjunct the Moon (even if neither is in the 2nd or 12th), a planet in the same sign as the Moon from the Ascendant, or the Full Moon birth (powerful Moon).

Cancellation of Kemadruma is very common — making the "uncancelled" version quite rare. When present and uncancelled, it indicates the need to develop emotional self-sufficiency and inner resilience.

Key phrases: Kemadruma Yoga, isolated Moon, struggle, cancellation, Bhangi, emotional independence.
