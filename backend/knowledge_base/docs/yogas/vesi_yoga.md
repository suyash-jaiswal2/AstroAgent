# Vesi and Voshi Yoga — Solar Planetary Influence

Vesi Yoga forms when any planet (other than the Moon) occupies the 2nd house from the Sun. Voshi Yoga forms when any planet occupies the 12th house from the Sun.

These yogas indicate that the solar self (identity, vitality, career) is closely informed and supported by the planet adjacent to the Sun.

Vesi Yoga with Jupiter in the 2nd from Sun: wisdom and eloquence support identity. With Venus: beauty and charm enhance the solar expression. With Saturn: discipline and seriousness shape the self-presentation.

Ubhayachari Yoga forms when planets occupy both the 2nd and 12th from the Sun simultaneously — considered highly auspicious for overall planetary support of the life purpose.

Key phrases: Vesi Yoga, Voshi Yoga, Ubhayachari, 2nd from Sun, 12th from Sun, solar influence, planets supporting Sun.
