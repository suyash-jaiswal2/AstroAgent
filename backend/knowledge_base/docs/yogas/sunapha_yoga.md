# Sunapha and Anapha Yoga — Planetary Support for the Moon

Sunapha Yoga forms when any planet (other than the Sun) is in the 2nd house from the Moon. Anapha Yoga forms when any planet is in the 12th from the Moon. These yogas indicate strong planetary support surrounding the Moon — the mind.

Sunapha with Jupiter: wisdom and generosity, wealth and good reputation. With Venus: artistic talent, charm, ease in relationships. With Mercury: sharp intellect, business acumen. With Mars: courage and self-reliance. With Saturn: discipline and sustained effort.

Durudhara Yoga forms when both the 2nd and 12th from the Moon are occupied — a particularly fortunate combination indicating full planetary support of the mind and emotions.

Key phrases: Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhara, 2nd from Moon, 12th from Moon, Moon support, planetary influence on mind.
