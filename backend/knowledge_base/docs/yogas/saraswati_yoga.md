# Saraswati Yoga — Knowledge and Creative Excellence

Saraswati Yoga forms when Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury are all placed in kendras or trikonas (1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, or 11th houses), with at least one of them in its own sign, exaltation, or friendly sign.

This yoga is named for Saraswati, the goddess of learning, music, and the arts. It produces exceptional intellectual gifts, artistic talent, wisdom, eloquence, and success in creative or educational fields.

The native with Saraswati Yoga is often multi-talented — skilled in music, writing, philosophy, and teaching simultaneously. The yoga requires the three planets to be strong; afflicted forms produce talent but with notable obstacles.

Key phrases: Saraswati Yoga, Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, kendras, trikonas, knowledge, creativity, arts, eloquence.
