# Yoga Karaka — The Most Benefic Planet in a Chart

A Yoga Karaka is a planet that simultaneously rules a kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) and a trikona (1st, 5th, 9th) house. For specific Ascendants, one planet becomes the chart's most powerful benefic.

For Taurus Ascendant: Saturn rules the 9th (trikona) and 10th (kendra) — making Saturn the Yoga Karaka. For Libra Ascendant: Saturn rules the 4th (kendra) and 5th (trikona). For Cancer Ascendant: Mars rules the 5th and 10th.

The Yoga Karaka planet is the most powerful benefic for that chart, regardless of its natural nature. Even malefic planets (Mars, Saturn) become powerful forces for good when they are Yoga Karaka.

During the Yoga Karaka planet's Mahadasha and Antardasha, the native typically experiences their greatest growth and achievement.

Key phrases: Yoga Karaka, kendra-trikona lord, Taurus ascendant Saturn, Cancer ascendant Mars, chart benefic, Dasha activation.
