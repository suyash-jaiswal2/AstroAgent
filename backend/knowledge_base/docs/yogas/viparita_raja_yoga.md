# Viparita Raja Yoga — Success Through Adversity

Viparita Raja Yoga (reversed Raj Yoga) forms when the lords of the Dusthana houses (6th, 8th, or 12th) are placed in other Dusthana houses, or when they are in mutual conjunction and neither occupies or aspects a kendra or trikona.

The paradox of this yoga is that it produces remarkable success precisely through adversity, loss, and challenges. The native may experience significant hardships but rises above them in unexpected ways — often through the downfall of enemies or the transformation of losses into gains.

This yoga is particularly powerful when the 8th lord is in the 12th or the 12th lord is in the 6th. It activates during the Dasha of the yoga-forming planets.

Key phrases: Viparita Raja Yoga, 6th lord, 8th lord, 12th lord, dusthana, success through adversity, reversal of fortune.
