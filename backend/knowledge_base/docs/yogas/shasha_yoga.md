# Shasha Yoga — Saturn Mahapurusha

Shasha Yoga forms when Saturn is placed in a kendra house (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th) in its own sign (Capricorn or Aquarius) or exaltation (Libra at 20°).

This yoga produces exceptional discipline, administrative ability, political power, and leadership in large organizations. The native rises through sustained effort and is capable of extraordinary endurance and structured achievement.

Shasha Yoga natives often work in government, law, administration, or industries related to Saturn (mining, construction, agriculture, elder care). They tend to achieve their greatest success after age 35, when Saturn's qualities have matured.

Challenges: the native may be overly serious, prone to detachment, or carry a heavy sense of responsibility. The yoga's full activation occurs during Saturn Mahadasha (19 years).

Key phrases: Shasha Yoga, Saturn Mahapurusha, kendra, Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra, discipline, administration, power.
