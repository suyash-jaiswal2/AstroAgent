# Gaja Kesari Yoga — Elephant and Lion

Gaja Kesari Yoga (the Elephant-Lion Yoga) forms when Jupiter is in the 1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house from the natal Moon. This is one of the most commonly found and celebrated yogas in Vedic astrology.

Gaja (elephant) symbolizes royal wisdom and memory; Kesari (lion) symbolizes noble courage and leadership. Together, they create a person of exceptional wisdom, social influence, and humanitarian impact.

Effects: the native is respected in their community, possesses sharp intellect, is generous and noble in character, and often achieves a leadership or advisory role. The yoga is strongest when Jupiter is in its own sign (Sagittarius/Pisces) or exaltation (Cancer), and when the Moon is strong (especially in Taurus or Cancer).

Detection rule: Jupiter's house number − Moon's house number = 0, 3, 6, or 9 (i.e., 1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th from Moon).

Key phrases: Gaja Kesari, Jupiter from Moon, angular, wisdom, leadership, community respect, nobility.
