# Ruchaka Yoga — Mars Mahapurusha

Ruchaka Yoga forms when Mars is placed in a kendra house (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th) in its own sign (Aries or Scorpio) or exaltation (Capricorn at 28°).

This yoga produces exceptional physical vitality, courage, competitive drive, and leadership in action-oriented fields. Natives with Ruchaka Yoga often excel in military service, sports, surgery, engineering, or competitive business.

Traditionally described character: athletic build, courageous, commanding, decisive, with natural martial skill. They are natural warriors who excel when there is a challenge to overcome.

The yoga is activated during Mars Mahadasha (7 years) and Antardasha, bringing peak physical performance and competitive success during those periods.

Key phrases: Ruchaka Yoga, Mars Mahapurusha, kendra, Aries, Scorpio, Capricorn, courage, competition, vitality.
