# Graha Malika Yoga — Planetary Chain

Graha Malika Yoga (garland of planets) forms when all seven classical planets occupy seven consecutive houses without any gap between them. This forms a continuous chain of planetary energy flowing through the chart.

The yoga indicates a person of complex, multi-dimensional experience — their life encompasses many different domains in a connected, sequential way. Fortune tends to manifest in waves, activating each house's themes as different Dasha periods unfold.

The strength of Graha Malika depends on which houses the chain occupies. A chain running through kendras and trikonas is significantly more powerful than one running through dusthanas.

Key phrases: Graha Malika, planetary chain, seven houses, consecutive, linked destiny, multi-dimensional.
