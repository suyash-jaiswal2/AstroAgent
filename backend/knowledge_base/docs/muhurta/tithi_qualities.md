# Tithi Qualities for Muhurta

The 30 tithis (lunar days) each have unique qualities for timing activities.

Excellent tithis: Pratipada (1st, new beginnings), Dwitiya (2nd, creative projects), Tritiya (3rd, learning and medicine), Panchami (5th, intellectual work), Saptami (7th, trade and vehicles), Dashami (10th, auspicious for most activities), Ekadashi (11th, fasting and spirituality), Trayodashi (13th, joy and success).

Inauspicious tithis (avoid major activities): Chaturthi (4th), Ashtami (8th, challenging), Navami (9th, avoid new starts), Chaturdashi (14th, before new/full moon — emotionally volatile), Amavasya (new moon, avoid for business).

Purnima (full moon) is generally auspicious for spiritual activity and community gatherings.

For business launches: Dwitiya, Tritiya, Panchami, Saptami, Dashami, Ekadashi, Trayodashi are strongly preferred.
