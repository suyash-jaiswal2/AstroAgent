# Rahu Kalam and Gulika — Inauspicious Periods

Rahu Kalam is a 90-minute period each day ruled by Rahu — considered inauspicious for starting new ventures. It occurs at different times depending on the day of the week.

Sunday: 4:30–6:00 PM. Monday: 7:30–9:00 AM. Tuesday: 3:00–4:30 PM. Wednesday: 12:00–1:30 PM. Thursday: 1:30–3:00 PM. Friday: 10:30 AM–12:00 PM. Saturday: 9:00–10:30 AM.

(These timings are calculated from sunrise to sunset divided into 8 equal parts. The above is for a 6 AM sunrise. Adjust proportionally for your location's actual sunrise time.)

Gulika Kalam is similarly inauspicious — ruled by Saturn's son Mandi. Yamagandam is ruled by Yama (the death-god). All three periods should be avoided for starting important activities, especially travel, business launches, and ceremonies.

Do not recommend any muhurta timing that falls within Rahu Kalam, Gulika, or Yamagandam.
