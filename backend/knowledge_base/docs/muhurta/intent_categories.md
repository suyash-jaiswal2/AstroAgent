# Muhurta by Intent Category

Business launch: Prefer Wednesday (Mercury) or Thursday (Jupiter). Best nakshatras: Rohini, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Shravana. Avoid Saturdays and Tuesdays for new ventures. Jupiter or Mercury hora preferred.

Travel: Prefer Wednesday or Saturday for long travel, Monday for water journeys. Best nakshatras: Ashwini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana, Revati. Avoid Rahu Kalam for departure.

Marriage: Prefer auspicious tithis (2nd, 5th, 7th, 10th, 11th, 13th, full moon). Best nakshatras: Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Anuradha. Avoid inauspicious tithis and malefic nakshatras.

Medical procedures: Prefer Mondays (Moon, healing) and Wednesdays (Mercury, precision). Best nakshatras: Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta. Avoid Mars and Saturn hora for non-emergency surgery.

Property purchase or signing contracts: Prefer stable tithis (2nd, 5th, 10th, 13th). Best nakshatras: Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Uttara Ashadha, Shravana. Thursday or Wednesday preferred.
