# Nakshatra Suitability by Activity

For travel: Ashwini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Hasta, Chitra, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati. Avoid Bharani, Krittika, Ashlesha, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Vishakha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Purva Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada.

For business launch: Rohini (most auspicious), Pushya (most auspicious), Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Chitra, Anuradha, Uttara Ashadha, Shravana, Dhanishtha, Uttara Bhadrapada, Revati.

For marriage: Rohini, Mrigashira, Magha, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Mula, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada, Revati.

For medical procedures: Ashwini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Pushya, Hasta, Chitra, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana.

Avoid for all major activities: Bharani, Krittika, Ardra, Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Shatabhisha. These are called Gandanta or Ashtottara-shashtyamsha nakshatras (harsh/sharp).
