# Compatibility Doshas — Critical Defects

Nadi Dosha: When both partners' Moon nakshatras belong to the same Nadi (Adi, Madhya, or Antya), Nadi Dosha occurs — traditionally associated with health problems in the relationship or offspring, and reduced marital harmony. Nadi receives 0 points when dosha is present. Cancellation: different sub-padas of the same nakshatra, or specific nakshatra-sign combinations.

Bhakoot Dosha: Certain Moon sign combinations score 0 — specifically the 2–12 axis (one partner's Moon is 2 signs from the other's) and the 6–8 axis. The 2–12 combination is associated with financial issues; 6–8 with health and conflict issues.

Gana Dosha: When a Rakshasa Gana person marries a Deva Gana person without matching, temperament differences can create ongoing friction. Manushya (human) Gana is generally compatible with both.

Note: Doshas should be assessed holistically. Many happy marriages have doshas; many difficult ones have high Ashtakoot scores. Other factors in the chart are equally important.
