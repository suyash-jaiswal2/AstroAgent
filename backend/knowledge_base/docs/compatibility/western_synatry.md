# Western Synastry — Aspect-Based Compatibility

Western synastry overlays two birth charts to identify aspects between Planet A in Chart 1 and Planet B in Chart 2. Key axes:

Sun–Moon aspects: fundamental compatibility of ego (Sun) and emotional nature (Moon). Conjunction or trine = natural harmony. Opposition = powerful attraction with tension.

Venus–Mars aspects: romantic and sexual chemistry. Conjunction = intense attraction. Trine = flowing, natural desire. Square = tension that creates chemistry but also friction.

Moon–Moon aspects: emotional compatibility. Same sign or trine = emotional resonance. Square or opposition = emotional friction requiring work.

Jupiter–Sun/Moon: one partner's Jupiter aspecting the other's Sun or Moon indicates a growth-enabling, supportive connection — one partner expands the other's life.

Saturn aspects: where Saturn from one chart aspects the other's planets, it brings long-term stabilizing influence but can also feel restricting. Saturn–Sun or Saturn–Moon in synastry often indicates a karmic, enduring connection.
