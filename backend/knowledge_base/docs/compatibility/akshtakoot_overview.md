# Ashtakoot Compatibility — The 36-Point System

Ashtakoot (eight factors) matching is the classical Vedic system for assessing compatibility, primarily based on the Moon nakshatras of both partners. Total score out of 36 points.

Score interpretation: 36/36 — exceptionally rare, perfect match. 28–35 — excellent, highly compatible. 21–27 — good, compatible with some areas of growth. 18–20 — average, requires mutual understanding. Below 18 — challenging, significant differences to navigate. Below 15 — very challenging; traditional astrology advises caution.

The eight kutas in order of importance: Nadi (8 pts), Bhakoot (7 pts), Gana (6 pts), Graha Maitri (5 pts), Yoni (4 pts), Tara (3 pts), Vashya (2 pts), Varna (1 pt).

Note: Ashtakoot is one tool in compatibility analysis. Final compatibility should consider full chart synastry, Dasha timings, Venus and Mars placement, and the 7th house of both charts.
