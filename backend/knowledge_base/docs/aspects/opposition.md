# Opposition — Polarity and Awareness

An opposition occurs when two planets are approximately 180° apart (orb: ±8°). It represents the polarization of two opposing forces, creating awareness through contrast. The tension of an opposition is often projected outward — experienced as challenges from others or the environment.

Oppositions involve signs of the same modality (two Cardinal, two Fixed, or two Mutable signs). The key is integration — bringing the two poles into conscious balance rather than swinging between extremes.

Saturn opposite Moon creates the tension between emotional need and structural reality. Jupiter opposite Saturn is the classic expansion vs. restriction opposition. Sun opposite Moon (Full Moon births) creates an inherently aware, socially attuned personality.

Key phrases: polarization, balance, projection, 180°, awareness through contrast, opposing forces, integration.
