# Conjunction — Planets United

A conjunction occurs when two planets occupy the same degree (orb: ±8°). It is the most powerful aspect, representing a complete merging of two planetary energies. The combined energy is intensified, focused, and operates as a single unified force.

Beneficial conjunctions (Jupiter-Venus, Sun-Mercury Budhaditya Yoga) amplify positive qualities. Challenging conjunctions (Saturn-Mars, Sun-Saturn) create tension between conflicting energies that must be consciously integrated.

In Vedic astrology, conjunction (Yuti) is considered one of the most powerful combinations. The closer the orb, the more complete the fusion. Planets within 1° of each other are inseparable in expression.

Key phrases: fusion, intensification, merging, unity, powerful combination, orb 8°, Yuti.
