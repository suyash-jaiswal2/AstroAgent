# Trine — Natural Gifts and Ease

A trine occurs when two planets are approximately 120° apart (orb: ±6°). It is the most harmonious aspect — representing natural talent, flowing energy, and areas of effortless ease and grace. Planets in trine share the same element (fire-fire, earth-earth, air-air, water-water).

Trines represent natural gifts that may be taken for granted. Jupiter trine Sun bestows natural confidence and opportunity. Venus trine Moon creates emotional warmth and creative ease. Mars trine Jupiter produces enthusiastic, confident action.

The shadow side of trines is complacency — their ease can lead to underutilization of natural gifts. Awareness transforms a trine from unconscious gift to conscious mastery.

Key phrases: natural talent, ease, harmony, 120°, flowing energy, same element, gifts, grace.
