# Square — Dynamic Tension and Growth

A square occurs when two planets are approximately 90° apart (orb: ±6°). It is the most challenging of the major aspects — representing friction, blockage, and the need for action to resolve internal conflict. Squares produce the greatest growth through their pressure.

Squares generate the motivation to take action and overcome obstacles. A natal square shows where we are compelled to work harder than others and where our greatest mastery ultimately develops.

Saturn square Sun challenges ego development through discipline. Mars square Moon creates emotional volatility that ultimately builds emotional strength. Jupiter square Saturn creates the tension between expansion and restraint.

Key phrases: tension, challenge, friction, 90°, growth through difficulty, dynamic action, blockage, conflict.
