# Sextile — Harmonious Opportunity

A sextile occurs when two planets are approximately 60° apart (orb: ±6°). It represents a harmonious, supportive relationship between the two planetary energies — opportunities that are available but require some conscious activation to utilize.

Unlike the trine (which flows automatically), the sextile's gifts must be actively engaged. It creates a productive cooperation between the two planetary principles, supporting skill development and practical opportunities.

Sextiles between benefics (Jupiter-Venus, Sun-Moon) create particularly supportive energy flows for creativity, relationships, and material growth.

Key phrases: opportunity, harmony, cooperation, 60°, skill, active engagement, supportive.
