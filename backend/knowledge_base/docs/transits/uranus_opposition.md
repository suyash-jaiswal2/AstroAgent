# Uranus Opposition — The Midlife Awakening

The Uranus opposition occurs around ages 40–42 when transiting Uranus opposes its natal position. It is one of the defining transits of the midlife, associated with the classic "midlife crisis" — the sudden, urgent need for authenticity, freedom, and change.

Themes: rejection of roles and identities that feel inauthentic, sudden career changes, relationship reassessments, return to abandoned passions, and a visceral sense that time is finite and must be used meaningfully.

The Uranus opposition is not a crisis — it is an awakening. The discomfort is proportional to how much authentic selfhood has been suppressed.

Key phrases: Uranus opposition, midlife awakening, age 40, authenticity, sudden change, freedom, identity crisis.
