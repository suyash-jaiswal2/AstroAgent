# Saturn Transit the Moon — Sade Sati

When Saturn transits over the natal Moon sign, through the preceding sign, and through the following sign, this 7.5-year period is called Sade Sati in Vedic astrology. It is the most watched and discussed transit in Jyotish.

Sade Sati themes: challenges, emotional heaviness, sense of burden, responsibilities increasing, delays, and enforced patience. However, it simultaneously brings deep maturation, karmic clearing, and often hidden spiritual growth. Many people accomplish their greatest work during Sade Sati.

The effects vary by: the natal Moon's sign and house, Saturn's natal strength, and the current Dasha running.

Key phrases: Sade Sati, Saturn transit Moon, 7.5 years, karmic clearing, emotional challenge, spiritual growth, discipline.
