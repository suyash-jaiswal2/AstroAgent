# Jupiter Return — Cycle of Expansion

Jupiter returns to its natal position every approximately 12 years — at ages 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, 84. Each Jupiter return initiates a new 12-year cycle of growth, opportunity, and expansion in the areas signified by Jupiter's natal placement.

Jupiter returns are generally positive transits — bringing new opportunities, expansion of horizons, and renewed optimism. The house Jupiter natally occupies determines where this new cycle of abundance begins.

Common experiences during Jupiter return: promotion, education opportunities, travel, marriages, births, philosophical awakenings, and financial improvement. The year of the Jupiter return is generally one of the more favorable years in a 12-year cycle.

Key phrases: Jupiter return, 12-year cycle, expansion, opportunity, age 12, 24, 36, 48, abundance, growth.
