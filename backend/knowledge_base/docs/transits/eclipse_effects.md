# Eclipses — Accelerated Karmic Gates

Solar and Lunar eclipses occur 4–6 times per year, always near the Rahu-Ketu nodal axis. They represent accelerated karmic gates — moments when the usual flow of time is punctuated by rapid, fated change.

Solar eclipses (New Moon on the nodal axis) seed new beginnings with karmic intensity. Their effects unfold over the following 6 months.

Lunar eclipses (Full Moon on the nodal axis) bring completions, revelations, and emotional culminations. Things hidden come to light.

When an eclipse falls on or near a natal planet or angle (within 3°), that year carries intensified themes related to that planet. Eclipse effects are strongest within 2 weeks of the eclipse date.

Key phrases: solar eclipse, lunar eclipse, nodal axis, karmic gate, accelerated change, 6-month effects, Rahu, Ketu.
