# Venus Retrograde — Relationship Review

Venus goes retrograde approximately every 18 months for about 40 days. During Venus retrograde, themes of love, relationships, beauty, and values come under profound review. Past relationships often resurface; heart matters that were buried come to the surface.

Common experiences: reconnection with past lovers, reassessment of current relationships, questioning of values and self-worth, changes in artistic taste, and financial review.

This is not the ideal time to begin new romantic relationships or make major purchases — but it is deeply valuable for reviewing and deepening existing ones.

Natal Venus retrograde in the birth chart indicates a person who revisits love themes repeatedly, seeks depth in relationships, and often has a non-conventional approach to partnership.

Key phrases: Venus retrograde, relationship review, past lovers, values reassessment, 18 months, love karma.
