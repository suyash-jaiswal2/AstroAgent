# Rahu-Ketu Axis Transit — Karmic Shift

The Rahu-Ketu nodal axis moves approximately 19.5 years for a complete cycle, transiting through a new sign every 18 months. Nodal transits to natal planets or angles are among the most transformative and karmic influences.

When Rahu transits your natal Ascendant, Moon, or Sun, it brings an intensification of desire, a major life shift, and often a sense of being pulled into unfamiliar territory. It amplifies whatever it touches.

When Ketu transits natal planets, it creates detachment, loss, and the sense that a chapter is ending. Ketu transits can feel disorienting but ultimately serve liberation and spiritual clarity.

The 18-month Rahu transit through any house brings obsessive focus to that life area. Major life events — moves, relationships, career changes — often cluster around nodal transits.

Key phrases: Rahu-Ketu transit, 18 months per sign, karmic shift, Rahu amplification, Ketu detachment, nodal axis.
