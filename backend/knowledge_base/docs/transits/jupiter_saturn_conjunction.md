# Jupiter-Saturn Conjunction — The Grand Conjunction

The Jupiter-Saturn conjunction occurs approximately every 20 years and marks the beginning of a new social and political era. Ancient astrologers considered it the most important of all planetary cycles — the meeting of the great chronocrators (time lords).

The 2020 conjunction in Aquarius at the winter solstice was particularly significant, heralding a shift from earth-sign conjunctions (material wealth focus) to air-sign conjunctions (technology, information, collective consciousness).

For the individual, a Jupiter-Saturn transit activates the tension between expansion and contraction, opportunity and responsibility, faith and structure. It marks chapters of genuine maturation where growth comes through disciplined effort.

Key phrases: grand conjunction, Jupiter-Saturn, 20-year cycle, social era, expansion meets structure, chronocrators.
