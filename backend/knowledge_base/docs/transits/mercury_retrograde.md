# Mercury Retrograde — Revision and Review

Mercury retrogrades 3–4 times per year for approximately 21 days each time. It is the most frequently discussed transit, associated with communication mishaps, technology glitches, travel delays, and contract confusion.

Mercury retrograde is best used for: revision, reflection, research, reconnection with old contacts, editing existing work, and completing unfinished communications.

Avoid during Mercury retrograde: signing major contracts, launching communications campaigns, making major technology purchases, or making important decisions based on incomplete information.

The shadow period (2 weeks before and after the retrograde station) also carries Mercury retrograde themes, though less intensely.

Natal Mercury retrograde indicates a deeply introspective, non-linear thinker whose greatest insights come through internal processing rather than quick responses.

Key phrases: Mercury retrograde, communication delays, technology glitches, revision, 3 times yearly, shadow period.
