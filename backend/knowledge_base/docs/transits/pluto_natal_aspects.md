# Pluto Transits — Total Transformation

Pluto transits are the most intense and irreversible of all planetary transits. When Pluto forms a major aspect (conjunction, square, opposition) to a natal planet, it brings total transformation to the themes of that planet — a death and rebirth process that leaves nothing unchanged.

Pluto transits can last 3–5 years, moving back and forth across the natal point in a series of passes. The themes are: power, control, elimination of what is no longer serving the soul's evolution, and the emergence of a completely transformed version of the natal planet's energy.

Pluto conjunct Sun: fundamental identity transformation. Pluto conjunct Moon: emotional rebirth. Pluto conjunct Ascendant: total life reinvention.

Key phrases: Pluto transit, total transformation, death and rebirth, intense, irreversible, 3–5 years, power and control.
