# Jupiter Transit the Ascendant — Expansion of Self

When Jupiter transits the natal Ascendant, it initiates a year of expansion, opportunity, and increased visibility. The personality becomes more confident and outward-reaching. New opportunities appear without heavy effort.

Physical expansion (weight gain) is common. The visibility and social reach of the person expands. New projects, relocations, and identity shifts are often initiated during this transit.

The house system of the natal chart determines which matters become Jupiter's channel of expansion. For a Sagittarius Ascendant, this transit is doubly powerful as Jupiter returns to its own territory.

Key phrases: Jupiter transit Ascendant, expansion, opportunity, visibility, new cycle, confidence, physical expansion.
