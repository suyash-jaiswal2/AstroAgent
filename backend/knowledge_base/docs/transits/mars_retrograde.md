# Mars Retrograde — Energy Turned Inward

Mars goes retrograde approximately every 26 months for about 60–80 days. During Mars retrograde, the planet's usual outward drive and assertiveness turn inward — making this a time for reviewing actions, resolving unfinished conflicts, and reconnecting with genuine motivation.

Challenges during Mars retrograde: frustration when action is blocked, reactivation of old conflicts, mechanical and physical energy delays, and difficulty with new initiatives launched during this period.

Opportunities: deep reflection on what we truly fight for, revisiting projects from the past, healing old anger, and strategic rather than reactive action.

Avoid: beginning major new enterprises, impulsive aggressive actions, or surgeries during Mars retrograde if possible.

Key phrases: Mars retrograde, energy review, past conflicts, strategic pause, 26 months, frustration, inner drive.
