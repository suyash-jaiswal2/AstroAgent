# Capricorn (Makara) — The Achiever

Element: Earth. Quality: Cardinal. Polarity: Feminine/Yin. Symbol: Sea Goat. Ruler: Saturn (Shani).

Capricorn represents ambition, discipline, structure, authority, and the achievement of long-term goals through persistent effort. It is the sign of the executive — pragmatic, strategic, and oriented toward mastery and status.

Core themes: ambition, discipline, structure, responsibility, authority, patience, career achievement, and the building of lasting legacies through hard work.

Strengths: disciplined, ambitious, reliable, strategic, hardworking, patient, excellent administrator. Challenges: cold, overly serious, workaholic, status-obsessed, pessimistic, rigid.

Mars is exalted in Capricorn (most powerful at 28°) — Mars's action and energy directed through Saturn's discipline produces exceptional executive and military ability. Jupiter is debilitated in Capricorn.

In Vedic astrology, Makara is a Chara (moveable) rashi. Body: knees, joints, bones. Capricorn Ascendant makes Saturn the chart ruler — disciplined, career-focused, and slowly achieving.

Key phrases: discipline, Saturn ruler, Mars exaltation, earth, cardinal, ambition, authority, structure, career, longevity.
