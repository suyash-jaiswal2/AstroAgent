# Scorpio (Vrischika) — The Transformer

Element: Water. Quality: Fixed. Polarity: Feminine/Yin. Symbol: Scorpion. Rulers: Mars (traditional), Pluto (modern).

Scorpio represents depth, intensity, transformation, hidden power, and the mysteries of death and rebirth. It is the sign of the investigator and transformer — penetrating, secretive, and profoundly capable of regeneration.

Core themes: transformation, depth, power, secrets, sexuality, research, occult knowledge, emotional intensity, and the capacity to die and be reborn psychologically.

Strengths: perceptive, powerful, magnetic, determined, research-oriented, emotionally deep, psychologically astute. Challenges: jealous, manipulative, secretive, controlling, vindictive, obsessive.

Ketu is exalted in Scorpio, making this sign particularly powerful for spiritual liberation and occult insight. The Moon is debilitated in Scorpio — emotional turbulence and karmic lessons in relationship to vulnerability.

In Vedic astrology, Vrischika is a Sthira (fixed) rashi. Body: reproductive organs, genitals, excretory system. Scorpio Ascendant makes Mars the chart ruler — intense, determined, and investigative.

Key phrases: transformation, Mars and Pluto rulers, water, fixed, depth, secrets, sexuality, Ketu exaltation, Moon debilitation.
