# Sagittarius (Dhanu) — The Philosopher

Element: Fire. Quality: Mutable. Polarity: Masculine/Yang. Symbol: Centaur/Archer. Ruler: Jupiter (Guru).

Sagittarius represents higher wisdom, adventure, philosophy, spiritual quest, and the expansive search for meaning. It is the sign of the philosopher and explorer — optimistic, freedom-loving, and eternally seeking the bigger picture.

Core themes: philosophy, higher education, spirituality, foreign travel, freedom, optimism, adventure, and the quest for truth and meaning beyond the mundane.

Strengths: philosophical, optimistic, adventurous, generous, honest, freedom-loving, wise, multicultural. Challenges: tactless, overconfident, commitment-phobic, restless, self-righteous, excessive.

Jupiter is in its own sign in Sagittarius — Dhanu being the Moolatrikona sign for Jupiter (0°–10°). This gives this sign a natural association with dharma, law, and spiritual teaching.

In Vedic astrology, Dhanu is a Dwiswabhava (dual) rashi. Body: thighs, hips. Sagittarius Ascendant makes Jupiter the chart ruler — philosophical, dharmic, expansive, often drawn to teaching.

Key phrases: philosophy, Jupiter ruler, fire, mutable, higher learning, adventure, freedom, dharma, spiritual quest.
