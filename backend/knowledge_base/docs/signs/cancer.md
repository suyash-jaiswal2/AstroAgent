# Cancer (Karka) — The Nurturer

Element: Water. Quality: Cardinal. Polarity: Feminine/Yin. Symbol: Crab. Ruler: Moon (Chandra).

Cancer represents nurturing, emotional depth, home, family, and the instinctive protective instinct. It is the sign of the nurturer — deeply feeling, tenaciously loyal, and powerfully connected to memory and the past.

Core themes: emotional security, home, family, nurturing, intuition, memory, protectiveness, and cycles of the past. Cancer energy is receptive, empathic, and deeply personal.

Strengths: nurturing, empathic, intuitive, protective, loyal, imaginative, strong memory. Challenges: moody, clingy, overly sensitive, passive-aggressive, fear-based, withdrawn.

Jupiter is exalted in Cancer (most powerful at 5°) — this produces exceptional wisdom combined with compassion and nurturing abundance. Mars is debilitated in Cancer, indicating the difficulty of assertive action in an emotionally charged environment.

In Vedic astrology, Karka is a Chara (moveable) rashi. Body: chest, breasts, stomach. Cancer Ascendant makes Moon the chart ruler — emotionally fluid, home-centered, deeply intuitive.

Key phrases: nurturing, Moon ruler, Jupiter exaltation, water, cardinal, home, family, intuition, memory.
