# Libra (Tula) — The Diplomat

Element: Air. Quality: Cardinal. Polarity: Masculine/Yang. Symbol: Scales. Ruler: Venus (Shukra).

Libra represents balance, justice, partnership, beauty, and the art of harmonious relationship. It is the sign of the diplomat — graceful, fair-minded, relationship-oriented, and deeply attuned to aesthetic refinement.

Core themes: balance, fairness, partnership, beauty, social grace, indecision, and the desire for harmony. Libra energy is relational, aesthetic, and fundamentally oriented toward the Other.

Strengths: diplomatic, fair, aesthetic, charming, social, cooperative, intelligent, peaceful. Challenges: indecisive, people-pleasing, conflict-avoidant, superficial, dependent on others.

Saturn is exalted in Libra (most powerful at 20°) — this combination of Saturn's discipline with Libra's justice creates exceptional capacity for law, administration, and fair-minded leadership. The Sun is debilitated in Libra.

In Vedic astrology, Tula is a Chara (moveable) rashi. Body: kidneys, lower back, lumbar region. Libra Ascendant makes Venus the chart ruler — charming, artistic, and deeply partnership-oriented.

Key phrases: balance, Venus ruler, Saturn exaltation, air, cardinal, partnership, justice, beauty, diplomacy.
