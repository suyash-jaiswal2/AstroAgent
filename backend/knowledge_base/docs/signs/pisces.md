# Pisces (Meena) — The Mystic

Element: Water. Quality: Mutable. Polarity: Feminine/Yin. Symbol: Two Fish. Rulers: Jupiter (traditional), Neptune (modern).

Pisces represents spirituality, compassion, transcendence, dreams, and the dissolution of boundaries. It is the sign of the mystic — deeply sensitive, empathic, and attuned to the invisible dimensions of life.

Core themes: spirituality, compassion, sacrifice, dreams, illusion, creativity, transcendence, and the return to the divine source. Pisces energy dissolves separation and opens into universal love and boundlessness.

Strengths: empathic, spiritual, artistic, compassionate, intuitive, imaginative, selfless, attuned to the sacred. Challenges: escapist, boundaries-weak, self-pitying, easily deceived, addictive tendencies, impractical.

Venus is exalted in Pisces (most powerful at 27°) — in this sign, love becomes unconditional and aesthetic appreciation reaches the transcendent. Mercury is debilitated in Pisces.

In Vedic astrology, Meena is a Dwiswabhava (dual) rashi and the last sign of the zodiac, representing liberation (moksha). Body: feet, lymphatic system. Pisces Ascendant makes Jupiter the chart ruler — spiritual, compassionate, and artistically gifted.

Key phrases: spirituality, Jupiter and Neptune rulers, water, mutable, compassion, dreams, Venus exaltation, moksha, transcendence.
