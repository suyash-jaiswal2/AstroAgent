# Leo (Simha) — The Royalty

Element: Fire. Quality: Fixed. Polarity: Masculine/Yang. Symbol: Lion. Ruler: Sun (Surya).

Leo represents creative self-expression, dignity, leadership, and the radiant heart's generosity. It is the sign of royalty — magnanimous, proud, and naturally commanding attention and admiration.

Core themes: self-expression, leadership, creativity, pride, generosity, performance, and the desire to be seen and celebrated. Leo energy is warm, dramatic, and magnetically compelling.

Strengths: confident, generous, warm-hearted, creative, loyal, dignified, natural leader, entertaining. Challenges: arrogant, domineering, attention-seeking, inflexible, ego-driven.

The Sun rules Leo — its own sign. Saturn is debilitated at 20° Libra but is weakly placed in general when opposing Leo themes. Neptune in Leo (historically) brought dramatic collective idealism.

In Vedic astrology, Simha is a Sthira (fixed) rashi. Body: heart, upper back, spine. Leo Ascendant makes the Sun the chart ruler — a natural authority, confident personality, seeking recognition.

Key phrases: leadership, Sun ruler, fire, fixed, creativity, royalty, dignity, self-expression, performance, magnanimity.
