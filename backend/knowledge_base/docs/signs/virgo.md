# Virgo (Kanya) — The Analyst

Element: Earth. Quality: Mutable. Polarity: Feminine/Yin. Symbol: Virgin. Ruler: Mercury (Budha).

Virgo represents discernment, analytical precision, service, health, and the refinement of details. It is the sign of the analyst and healer — meticulous, practical, devoted to improvement, and skilled in separating the pure from the impure.

Core themes: analysis, precision, health, service, perfectionism, criticism, problem-solving, and the mastery of craft through detail and discernment.

Strengths: analytical, precise, service-oriented, intelligent, health-conscious, hardworking, reliable, skilled. Challenges: overcritical, anxious, perfectionist, worrisome, uptight, small-minded.

Mercury is exalted in Virgo (at 15°) and in its own sign — its strongest placement. Venus is debilitated in Virgo at 27°, indicating difficulty with free-flowing love and pleasure in this analytical sign.

In Vedic astrology, Kanya is a Dwiswabhava (dual) rashi. Body: intestines, lower abdomen, digestive system. Virgo Ascendant makes Mercury the chart ruler — a practical, service-oriented, health-conscious personality.

Key phrases: analysis, Mercury exaltation and ruler, earth, mutable, service, health, precision, discernment, craft.
