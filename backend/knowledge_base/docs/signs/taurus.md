# Taurus (Vrishabha) — The Builder

Element: Earth. Quality: Fixed. Polarity: Feminine/Yin. Symbol: Bull. Ruler: Venus (Shukra).

Taurus represents stability, material security, sensory pleasure, and patient persistence. It is the sign of the builder — methodical, determined, and deeply connected to the physical world and its pleasures.

Core themes: material security, beauty, comfort, patience, stubbornness, loyalty, and sensory experience. Taurus energy builds slowly but with exceptional durability.

Strengths: reliable, patient, practical, artistic, sensual, financially savvy, loyal. Challenges: stubborn, possessive, resistant to change, overindulgent, materialistic.

Moon is exalted in Taurus (most powerful at 3°) — this placement produces emotional stability and a calm, grounded nature. Rahu is also exalted in Taurus, indicating worldly desire and material accumulation.

In Vedic astrology, Vrishabha is a Sthira (fixed) rashi. Body parts: face, throat, neck. The Taurus Ascendant makes Venus the chart ruler — naturally artistic, comfort-loving, and security-oriented.

Key phrases: stability, Venus ruler, Moon exaltation, earth, fixed, beauty, material security, sensory pleasure, Rahu exaltation.
