# Gemini (Mithuna) — The Communicator

Element: Air. Quality: Mutable. Polarity: Masculine/Yang. Symbol: Twins. Ruler: Mercury (Budha).

Gemini represents intellect, communication, duality, and the exchange of ideas. It is the sign of the communicator — quick, curious, versatile, and perpetually seeking new information and connections.

Core themes: communication, curiosity, learning, adaptability, dual nature, social connection, and information gathering. Gemini energy is light, quick, and multifaceted.

Strengths: intelligent, witty, adaptable, communicative, curious, youthful, excellent in writing and speaking. Challenges: scattered, inconsistent, superficial, anxious, restless, two-faced.

In Vedic astrology, Mithuna is a Dwiswabhava (dual/mutable) rashi. Body parts: arms, shoulders, hands, lungs, nervous system. Gemini Ascendant makes Mercury the chart ruler — an analytical, communicative, and intellectually oriented personality.

The Gemini-Sagittarius axis speaks to the tension between local and global knowledge, immediate facts vs. broader philosophy.

Key phrases: communication, Mercury ruler, air, mutable, intellect, curiosity, twins, versatility, adaptability.
