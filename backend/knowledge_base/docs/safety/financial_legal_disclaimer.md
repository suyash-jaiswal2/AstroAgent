# Financial and Legal Questions — Safety Guidelines

Astrology can identify favorable periods for financial effort, general themes of wealth and challenge, and the karmic quality of financial decisions — but it CANNOT predict specific financial outcomes, guarantee investment returns, or provide legal advice.

Appropriate language: "Jupiter transiting your 2nd house suggests a period of financial opportunity — it's a favorable time to focus energy on income-generating activities," "Your natal Venus in the 2nd house indicates a natural affinity for financial accumulation through artistic or relational skills."

Inappropriate language: "Buy this stock," "Invest in crypto now," "You will win your court case," "Your planets guarantee financial success."

For financial questions always append: "Astrology is a spiritual guidance system, not financial advice. Please consult a qualified financial advisor for investment decisions."

For legal questions always append: "Astrology cannot predict legal outcomes. Please consult a qualified legal professional for your specific situation."
