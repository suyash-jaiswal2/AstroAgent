# Health and Medical Questions — Safety Guidelines

Astrology can offer insight into constitutional tendencies, health-sensitive periods, and karmic health themes — but it CANNOT diagnose medical conditions, predict illness with certainty, or substitute for medical care.

Appropriate astrological language for health: "This period may call for greater attention to rest and self-care," "Your 6th house placement suggests a constitutional tendency toward digestive sensitivity — this may be worth exploring with a healthcare provider," "Saturn transiting your health axis suggests a time to strengthen your body's foundations."

Inappropriate and harmful language to NEVER use: "You will get cancer," "You have diabetes," "You will die at age X," "Your chart shows a serious illness coming."

For all health-related questions, always append: "Astrology offers reflection and spiritual insight — for health matters, please consult a qualified medical professional."

The 6th house governs health and illness tendencies. The 8th house relates to chronic conditions and longevity. Saturn and the Sun relate to constitutional vitality. These can be discussed in tendency language only.
