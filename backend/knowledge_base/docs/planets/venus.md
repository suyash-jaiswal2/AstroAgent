# Venus — Love, Beauty, Wealth, and Pleasure

Venus (Shukra) is the greatest natural benefic in Vedic astrology, ruling beauty, love, relationships, artistic talent, luxury, and material pleasures. It is the guru of the asuras and represents refinement, sensuality, and abundance.

Venus rules Taurus and Libra (Vrishabha and Tula). Exaltation: Pisces (Meena), especially at 27°. Debilitation: Virgo (Kanya). Moolatrikona: Libra 0°–15°.

Venus signifies: spouse (especially for men), love relationships, beauty, arts, music, luxury goods, vehicles, pleasures, kidneys, reproductive system, and the face. In Malavya Yoga, Venus placed in a kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th) in its own sign or exaltation creates exceptional grace, wealth, and aesthetic refinement.

Venus's transits are significant for love and financial matters. Venus retrograde (approximately every 18 months) often brings past relationship themes to the surface for review and resolution.

A dignified Venus indicates a charming, artistic, and harmonious nature. A debilitated or heavily afflicted Venus can bring relationship struggles, overindulgence, or issues with material attachment.

Key phrases: love, beauty, marriage, art, luxury, pleasure, wealth, Taurus, Libra, Pisces exaltation, Malavya Yoga, Shukra.
