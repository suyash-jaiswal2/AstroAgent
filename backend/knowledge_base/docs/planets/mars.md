# Mars — Energy, Courage, and Drive

Mars (Mangal, Kuja) is the natural malefic of fire, energy, courage, ambition, and conflict. It represents the warrior archetype — physical strength, decisive action, passion, and the capacity to fight for what one values. Mars rules Aries and Scorpio (Mesha and Vrischika).

Exaltation: Capricorn (Makara), especially at 28°. Debilitation: Cancer (Karka). Moolatrikona: Aries 0°–12°.

Mars signifies: brothers, younger siblings, military, police, surgery, engineers, blood, muscles, head, and accidents. Mangal Dosha (also called Kuja Dosha) occurs when Mars is placed in the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house — traditionally believed to affect marriage harmony and requiring matching with a partner who also has Mangal Dosha.

Ruchaka Yoga — Mars in a kendra in its own sign or exaltation — produces exceptional physical vitality, leadership, and competitive success.

Mars transits last approximately 6–8 weeks per sign and bring action, assertiveness, and potential conflict to the house they occupy. Mars retrograde (every ~26 months) is a time to review impulsive actions and recalibrate energy expenditure.

Key phrases: energy, courage, conflict, brother, surgery, Aries, Scorpio, Capricorn exaltation, Mangal Dosha, Ruchaka Yoga.
