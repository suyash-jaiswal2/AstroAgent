# Saturn — Discipline, Karma, and Transformation

Saturn (Shani) is the taskmaster of the zodiac — the great timekeeper who teaches through limitation, delay, and discipline. Its lessons are karmic, often revealing where we must develop patience, responsibility, and mastery.

Saturn rules Capricorn and Aquarius (Makara and Kumbha). Exaltation: Libra (Tula), especially at 20°. Debilitation: Aries (Mesha). Moolatrikona: Aquarius 0°–20°.

Saturn signifies: longevity, service, hard work, restrictions, servants, elderly people, bones, teeth, joints, and chronic conditions. The Saturn return at approximately age 29–30 and 58–59 are the most significant transits in any life — times of intense maturation and life restructuring.

Sade Sati — Saturn's 7.5-year transit through the Moon sign and the two adjacent signs — is traditionally associated with challenges and transformation, though it also brings deep spiritual growth and karmic clearing.

Shasha Yoga — Saturn in a kendra in its own sign or exaltation — produces exceptional discipline, administrative ability, and long-lasting success.

Key phrases: karma, discipline, restriction, longevity, Saturn return, Sade Sati, Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra exaltation, Shasha Yoga.
