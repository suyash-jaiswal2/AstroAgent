# Rahu and Ketu — The Karmic Nodes

Rahu (North Node) and Ketu (South Node) are shadow planets (Chaya Grahas) — mathematical points where the Moon's orbit crosses the ecliptic. They are always exactly opposite each other in the chart and are among the most important placements in Vedic astrology.

Rahu represents worldly desire, obsession, illusion, foreign influences, technology, and the future direction of karmic growth. It amplifies whatever it touches. Rahu in a chart shows where the soul is being pulled forward — areas of intense desire, fascination, and new karmic experience.

Ketu represents past life mastery, detachment, spirituality, liberation (moksha), and the wisdom already earned. It creates disinterest and detachment in the house it occupies, but profound inner wisdom in those areas.

The Rahu-Ketu axis (also called the Nodal Axis) reveals the soul's karmic journey. The Rahu Mahadasha (18 years) and Ketu Mahadasha (7 years) in the Vimshottari Dasha system are among the most transformative life periods.

Ketu Exaltation: Scorpio. Rahu Exaltation: Taurus. Both are considered intensely malefic but are essential karmic teachers.

Key phrases: karmic axis, past lives, Rahu desire, Ketu detachment, moksha, Nodal axis, Rahu Mahadasha, shadow planets, obsession, liberation.
