# Mercury — Intellect, Communication, and Commerce

Mercury (Budha) is the planet of intellect, communication, analytical thinking, writing, commerce, and nervous system. It is a dual-natured planet — neither purely benefic nor malefic — adapting its expression to the planets it associates with. Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo in both systems.

Exaltation: Virgo (Kanya), especially at 15°. Debilitation: Pisces (Meena). Moolatrikona: Virgo 16°–20°.

Mercury signifies: speech, writing, logic, siblings, education, trade, skin, nervous system, and short-distance travel. Budhaditya Yoga — Mercury conjunct the Sun — is one of the most common and beneficial yogas, enhancing intelligence and communication skills.

When Mercury is retrograde (3 times per year, ~3 weeks each), communications, contracts, and technology can be prone to delays and misunderstandings. Natal retrograde Mercury often indicates a deeply reflective, non-linear mind.

Mercury is fastest when near the Sun (combust within 14°). Combustion weakens Mercury's clarity but can also intensify intellectual focus inward.

Key phrases: intellect, communication, writing, siblings, commerce, Gemini, Virgo, logic, nervous system, Budhaditya Yoga.
