# Neptune — Spirituality, Illusion, and Dissolution

Neptune is the planet of the transcendent — spirituality, mysticism, dreams, compassion, and dissolution of boundaries. Discovered in 1846, it spends approximately 14 years in each sign and operates at the collective, unconscious level.

In Western astrology, Neptune rules Pisces (co-ruling with Jupiter). It dissolves boundaries between self and other, between the material and spiritual. Neptune heightens psychic sensitivity and artistic imagination but can also manifest as confusion, deception, or escapism.

Neptune signifies: spirituality, dreams, meditation, music, film, photography, addictions, illusions, hospitals, retreat, and self-undoing. When prominently placed (especially conjunct the Ascendant or Sun), Neptune produces deeply spiritual, creative, and empathic individuals.

Neptune transits are slow and generational. A natal Neptune-Sun or Neptune-Moon aspect can indicate a profound spiritual sensitivity that blurs the boundary between personal will and universal flow.

In Vedic astrology, Neptune is not part of the classical system. Its influence is considered by modern Jyotish practitioners but it carries no Dasha lordship.

Key phrases: spirituality, illusion, compassion, dreams, addiction, dissolution, Pisces, transcendence, psychic, generational.
