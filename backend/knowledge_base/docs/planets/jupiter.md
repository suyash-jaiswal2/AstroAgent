# Jupiter — Wisdom, Expansion, and Grace

Jupiter (Guru, Brihaspati) is the greatest benefic in all astrological traditions. It represents wisdom, higher learning, philosophy, dharma, abundance, and divine grace. Jupiter is the preceptor of the gods — the teacher who guides toward truth and meaning.

Jupiter rules Sagittarius and Pisces (Dhanu and Meena). Exaltation: Cancer (Karka), especially at 5°. Debilitation: Capricorn (Makara). Moolatrikona: Sagittarius 0°–10°.

Jupiter signifies: children, guru, wisdom, philosophy, law, higher education, foreign lands, liver, hips, thighs, and fat tissue. Gaja Kesari Yoga — Jupiter in a kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from the Moon — is one of the most auspicious yogas, bestowing wisdom, social prominence, and leadership.

Hamsa Yoga — Jupiter in its own sign or exaltation in a kendra — creates exceptional wisdom, spiritual authority, and righteous leadership.

Jupiter transits last approximately one year per sign. Jupiter's transit over the natal Moon (Guru Chandal, if with Rahu, or otherwise benefic) is highly significant. Jupiter return (approximately age 12, 24, 36, 48) marks major life expansions.

Key phrases: wisdom, abundance, children, guru, dharma, expansion, Sagittarius, Pisces, Cancer exaltation, Gaja Kesari, Hamsa Yoga.
