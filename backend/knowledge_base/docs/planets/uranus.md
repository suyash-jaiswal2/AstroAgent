# Uranus — Innovation, Rebellion, and Awakening

Uranus is a transpersonal planet, discovered in 1781 and associated with sudden change, revolution, technology, innovation, and liberation from the old order. It spends approximately 7 years in each sign, coloring entire generations with its themes.

In Western astrology, Uranus rules Aquarius (co-ruling with Saturn). It represents the urge to break free from convention, the flash of genius, humanitarian ideals, and sudden disruptions that ultimately serve awakening.

Uranus signifies: technology, electricity, internet, astrology itself, sudden reversals of fortune, rebelliousness, eccentricity, and collective evolution. When Uranus transits a sensitive natal point, it brings unexpected change — a sudden shift that cannot be reversed.

In Vedic astrology (Jyotish), Uranus is not traditionally used but is increasingly incorporated by modern practitioners. It does not have an assigned Dasha period.

The Uranus opposition at approximately age 40–42 is a key life transit — the midlife awakening that prompts a fundamental reassessment of authenticity and personal freedom.

Key phrases: innovation, rebellion, technology, awakening, sudden change, Aquarius, midlife crisis, generational, transpersonal.
