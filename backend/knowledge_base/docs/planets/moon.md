# The Moon — Mind, Emotions, and the Mother

The Moon is the most important planet in Vedic astrology (Jyotish) — more significant even than the Sun for daily experience. It represents the mind (manas), emotions, instincts, memory, and the mother. The Moon sign at birth (Chandra Lagna) is used as the primary Ascendant for Vedic predictions.

The Moon rules Cancer (Karka). Exaltation: Taurus (Vrishabha) — especially powerful at 3°. Debilitation: Scorpio (Vrischika). It moves approximately 13° per day, spending about 2.25 days in each sign.

The Moon signifies: mother, nourishment, home, emotional security, public, masses, water, breasts, lungs, and the left eye. Its nakshatra placement is critical in Vedic astrology for Dasha calculations — the birth Moon nakshatra determines the starting Vimshottari Dasha.

A strong Moon in Taurus, Cancer, or well-aspected produces emotional stability, nurturing capacity, and mental clarity. A Moon in Scorpio, aspected by Saturn or Rahu, can indicate emotional turbulence or karmic emotional lessons.

The Moon's phase at birth (waxing or waning, full or new) significantly modifies its strength. A full Moon is extremely powerful; a new Moon shares its energy with the Sun.

Key phrases: mind, mother, emotions, instinct, home, public, nourishment, Cancer, Taurus exaltation, Dasha lord.
