# The Sun — Soul, Identity, and Vitality

The Sun is the central luminary of the chart and represents the core self, ego, willpower, and conscious identity. It is the soul's purpose and the source of vitality. In Vedic astrology, the Sun (Surya) is a natural malefic but a royal planet — the king of the cosmos. It rules Leo in the Western system and is also the lord of Leo (Simha) in Jyotish.

The Sun signifies: father, authority figures, government, career through recognition, leadership, bone structure, heart, and eyes. Its house position shows where we seek to shine, be seen, and assert our identity.

Exaltation: Aries (Mesh). Debilitation: Libra (Tula). Own sign: Leo (Simha). Moolatrikona: Leo 0°–20°.

A strong Sun produces confidence, leadership, and vitality. A weak or afflicted Sun can indicate ego struggles, lack of recognition, or health issues related to the heart and eyes.

Transits of the Sun last approximately one month per sign and illuminate the house they pass through, bringing attention and activity to that life area.

Key phrases: soul purpose, father, authority, recognition, vitality, government, ego, leadership, Leo, Aries exaltation.
