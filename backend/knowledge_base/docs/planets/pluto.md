# Pluto — Power, Transformation, and Regeneration

Pluto is the planet of death, rebirth, and profound transformation. Discovered in 1930, it spends 12–30 years in each sign (moving slowest in Taurus, fastest in Scorpio) and represents the deepest collective undercurrents of power and change.

In Western astrology, Pluto rules Scorpio (co-ruling with Mars). It governs the underworld — all that is hidden, taboo, or must be destroyed before it can be reborn. Plutonian themes include: power dynamics, inheritance, death, sexuality, psychology, and the transformation of shadow into light.

Pluto signifies: intense transformation, power and its abuse, obsession, secrets, inheritances, depth psychology, and collective upheaval. Pluto transits to natal planets are among the most intense astrological experiences — total deconstruction followed by renewal.

When Pluto was in Capricorn (2008–2024), it transformed institutional structures globally. Its entry into Aquarius (2024–2043) signals a generation-defining transformation of technology and collective power.

In Vedic astrology, Pluto is not part of the classical system.

Key phrases: transformation, power, death and rebirth, Scorpio, shadow, intensity, generational, collective, underworld.
